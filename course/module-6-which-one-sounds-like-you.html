Last one. What would have to be true for you to take one?

Nobody grades this. Naming the thing that’s still unresolved is usually the fastest way to resolve it.

Take this with you

  • 01The car arrives locked. Unlock from the app or the screen, then open the door — and buckle up. Seatbelts are required the whole ride.
  • 02Start the ride from the in-car screen. It runs your route, the music, and the temperature.
  • 03Tap Support on the screen or in the app to reach a real person — any time, for any reason.
  • 04If the car is unsure, it slows or pulls over instead of guessing. That’s the design working, not a fault.
  • 05At your stop, open the doors from the screen — and close them behind you.

These five are also in the printable course document — along with a few things worth remembering for a first ride, if you ever want them.
