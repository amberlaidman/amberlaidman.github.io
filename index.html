About

Building learning that respects the person on the other end of it.

My work in L&D has been shaped by years spent inside frontline operations — retail telecom, contact center operations, and hospitality. That background changes how I design: learning has to earn attention, not demand it, and hold up under real workflow, real time constraints, and real days that don't stop.
