Budget Book

Your own private place to keep track of bills, spending and debt with Claude.

Here's how to get your own copy of the budget page I use. It starts out empty, so the first time you open it, you'll spend a little while adding your bills and income.

There are seven steps, and you can select any step to open it.

Steps

1How it works

Budget Book is a page that lives in your own Claude account. It starts out empty, and it's only as useful as what you put in, so getting started has two parts.

  1. Get your copy. In step 4, you download a template, and Claude turns it into your own private page.
  2. Add your information. The first time you open your page, a Getting started list walks you through your income, bills, credit cards and a goal. Amounts can be rough, and you can fix them later.

Once that's done, here's what you can use it for:

Overview
Your share of bills for the month, a month card (income minus bills, card minimums and an everyday spending plan), and the next few things coming due.
Expenses
Your bills and subscriptions with amount, how often, due day and category. Tick a bill paid each month, or switch to a calendar to look at other months.
Check-in and Trends
A monthly check-in that compares what you planned with what you spent. You can also upload card files (CSV, PDF or a screenshot) to see spending by category and your top places.
Money
Income and payments received, credit card debt with a payoff order, savings goals and salary history.
Settings
Optional bill splitting with a partner or roommate, renaming categories, hiding tabs you don't use, Excel download and backup.

A quick, honest note: this is a tracking tool, not financial advice. The numbers are only what you enter or approve, and when Claude reads a statement or screenshot it can misread something, so you check a preview before anything is saved. It's built so it never fills in an amount you didn't give it.

2Before you start

Check these few things first so setup goes smoothly:

  • A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Your page saves your data and can ask Claude to read statements, and I set mine up on a paid plan. Not confirmed: whether every part works on the free plan, and plans and features can vary by country.
  • A regular chat, not incognito. Incognito chats can't create files, so they can't make your page.
  • "Artifacts," "AI-powered artifacts" and "Cloud code execution and file creation" turned on. They're usually on already, and you can check in Settings → Capabilities.
  • A computer for setup, if you can. You'll download one file and attach it to a chat, which is easiest on a computer, and after that your page works on your phone too.
  • A rough list of your bills, cards and income. A bank or card website, a statement or a spreadsheet works. You don't need exact amounts to begin.
3Your privacy choices

Your page will hold money information, so it's worth deciding a few things before you start.

Before you paste the prompt

  1. How your chats may be used. You choose whether your chats help improve Claude. Use the Model Improvement setting in your Privacy Settings, and turn it off first if you prefer.
  2. Memory. If you'd rather keep this setup out of Claude's memory, turn off "Generate memory from chats" in the Memory section of your settings before you start. Don't use an incognito chat for this, because incognito can't make your page.
  3. What to leave out. The page doesn't need account numbers, logins or your Social Security number, so please don't type or upload them.

After setup

  • Your page starts private. Only you can open it until you share it, and I'd keep the link to yourself since it shows your money.
  • Your saved data is set to owner only. The starter prompt asks Claude to set up your page so only you can read or change what's saved in it. Not confirmed: I haven't tested what someone else sees if you share the link.
  • Statements and screenshots go to Claude. A CSV file is read in your browser, but reading a PDF or a screenshot uses Claude, so that content is handled like anything else you share in a chat. Not confirmed: exactly which parts are sent, so if you'd rather not share a statement, type the numbers in instead.
  • Your data is yours. Use Download a backup or the Excel download in your page's Settings to save a copy.

From Claude's Help Center and Privacy Center. Links are in the Help tab.

4Get your copy

You'll download a blank copy of my Budget Book, with none of my information in it, and Claude turns it into your own private page.

  1. Download the template. Your browser may ask you to confirm first, and the file is called budget-book-template.html. You don't need to open this file, since you'll attach it to a chat in point 3 below.
    Download not working? Open the file to save it

  2. Copy the starter prompt. You can read the full prompt below first if you'd like.

  3. Open a new regular chat in Claude, attach the template file, and paste the prompt in.
    Open Claude
  4. Open your new page. Claude sends you a link, and it's worth bookmarking since that's where you'll go every time.
  5. Allow Claude to help on the page. The first time you upload a statement or ask for suggestions, Claude asks for permission, so select Allow.

<how_to_work_with_me>
- Don't add any of my financial details to the file. The page asks for them itself and saves them privately.

  • Want to see it first? The Demo tab has a working copy with made-up numbers you can load and play with.
  • "Not confirmed:" means it's a guess. When Claude isn't sure, it starts the sentence that way, so check those details yourself.
5Your first visit

The first time you open your page, it shows a Getting started list with a progress bar. It goes away once everything is done, or you can select Hide this list.

  1. Choose whether you split bills with someone. This is optional. Leave it off if you pay your own bills.
  2. Add your income. A paycheck, unemployment or side work. Amounts can be rough.
  3. Add your bills and subscriptions. Rent, phone, streaming, insurance. Give each a due day if you know it, and the calendar uses it.
  4. Add credit cards. Balances you carry, or select I don't have any.
  5. Set a goal. Something to save for, or what you want the budget to do.

About the starter content: the page has no amounts of its own. The categories (like housing or subscriptions) are only starting names, and you can rename them in Settings.

6Using it week to week

There's no right way to use it, but here's a rhythm that works well:

  • When a bill is paid, open Expenses and tick its circle. Use the calendar view to look ahead or to pay ahead for another month.
  • Glance at Overview to see the month card and the top three things coming up, with a button for the rest.
  • Once a month, do the Check-in to compare planned and actual spending.
  • When you have a card file, open Trends and upload a CSV, PDF or screenshot. You see a preview first, and nothing is saved until you approve it.
  • When your income changes, add it under Money, along with card balances and savings progress.

Once a month: open Settings in your page and select Download a backup, and if anything ever goes wrong, you can put it back with the restore option there. Not confirmed: I've only tested restoring a backup on my own page.

7Changes and updates

To change your page, go back to the chat where you made it (or start a new one and paste your page's link) and ask Claude. For example:

  • "Rename the categories on my Budget Book to match how I think about my money."
  • "Add a tab to my Budget Book for [something you track]."
  • "Change my Budget Book's colors to something calmer."

When I share a newer version of the template, you can update your page without losing anything. Download a backup first, then attach the new file to the chat where you made your page and ask Claude, "Publish this new version to my existing Budget Book page, at the same link, with the same capabilities as before." Your saved data stays with the page.

Not confirmed: I've only tested updates on my own page so far.

Troubleshooting

Select a question to open the answer.

Setup

The template didn't download.
Downloads ask you to confirm first, so look for a prompt and select Save. If nothing happens, select Download not working? Open the file to save it and save the page from your browser, or ask the person who shared this guide to send you the file.
Claude says it can't make the page.
Check that you're on a paid plan, in a regular chat (not incognito), and that "Artifacts," "AI-powered artifacts" and "Cloud code execution and file creation" are on in Settings → Capabilities. Then start a new chat, attach the file again and paste the prompt.
Claude wants to rebuild or change the page.
Reply, "Please publish the attached file exactly as it is, without changes." The page already does everything; it only needs to be published.

Using your page

Uploads or suggestions say Claude's features aren't available.
Open your page in the Claude app or on claude.ai, signed in to your own account. The first time you use them, Claude asks for permission to help on the page. Select Allow. If you declined by mistake, reload the page and try again.
A statement or screenshot was read wrong.
You see a preview before anything is saved, so fix or remove the lines that look wrong there. A CSV file is usually the most reliable, if your card website offers one.
I've reached a usage limit.
Reading files and getting suggestions use your own Claude plan. Wait a while and try again. Entering numbers by hand doesn't use it.
Something I saved is missing.
Reload the page first. If it's still missing, open Settings in your page and use the restore option with your latest backup.
The categories or tabs don't fit me.
Rename categories or hide tabs in Settings. For anything bigger, ask Claude. Step 7 of the Guide has example messages you can copy.
Still stuck? Ask Claude for help

Claude can help you troubleshoot. Go back to the chat where you made your page, since that chat already knows your setup. If you can't find it, start a new chat and include your page's link. Copy the message below, fill in the parts in brackets, and send it. Please leave out account numbers and other private details.

Help message

My Budget Book page isn't working the way I expected.

My page's link: [paste your page's link here if this is a new chat]
What I was trying to do: [for example, upload a card statement]
What happened instead: [describe it, or paste the exact message you got]

Please help me troubleshoot this one step at a time, in simple words.

There's more help inside your page, too, on its Help page. For account or billing questions, visit Claude's Help Center.

Your privacy choices are in step 3 of the Guide: Model Improvement, memory, what to leave out, and who can see your page. It's best to make them before you paste the prompt.

The demo on this page keeps whatever you type in this browser tab only. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere, and Start blank or Load sample data replaces it.

This guide counts visits and button clicks to see how it's used. It doesn't use cookies or collect personal details, and nothing is tracked inside your own Budget Book page.
