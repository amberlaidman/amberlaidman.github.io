Riding With No Driver

What it’s actually like to ride in a car that drives itself — and whether you’d get in one.

By the end, you should be able to

  • —Say what the car does when it isn’t sure what to do.
  • —Describe what the safety evidence shows — and what it doesn’t cover.
  • —Walk through a first ride, from hailing the car to closing the door.
  • —Decide, with a clearer picture, whether you’d take one.

Six modules · about 12 minutes · no quiz · read-aloud available

About the evidence

Safety figures, crash comparisons and the incidents named in Module 4 are drawn from published Waymo and Swiss Re research and from public NHTSA records. This is a fast-moving field — figures get revised and new incidents occur. Treat the numbers as accurate to the review date, not as current.
