About

A career at the intersection of training design, program management, and frontline enablement.

I think about learning the way I think about any experience worth making — every part matters, and so does how they fit together.

My work in L&D has been shaped by years spent inside frontline operations — retail telecom, contact center operations, and hospitality. That background changes how I design: learning has to feel worth showing up for, and hold up under real workflow, time constraints, and days that don't stop.

I work across instructional design, program management, and learning strategy — which usually means I'm holding the whole thing at once: what needs to be built, how it'll get delivered, and whether it'll actually land. I've built custom interactive courses from scratch, designed and delivered onboarding for over 100 employees globally and across industries, and led training sessions for teams across regions.

More recently, I've been teaching myself to vibe code well enough to build interactive learning experiences that don't exist in any standard authoring tool — pH-responsive sliders, custom stepped scenarios, live embedded demos. It's the kind of work I get fully immersed in until the thing is built. The gin course on the Work page is the most complete version of that experiment so far.

Outside work: photography, travel, running, and figuring out how to make things I couldn't build before.
