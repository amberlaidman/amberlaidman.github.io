About

Twenty years across spirits, telecom, and travel.

I design learning around the human doing the job — what they need to know, what they need to do, and how to make the path from one to the other feel obvious instead of obligatory.

I've spent my career in learning and development across industries where the work happens on the floor — retail telecom, contact center operations, hospitality, and now spirits. That background shapes how I design: learning has to survive contact with real workflow, real time constraints, and real humans who didn't sign up for a course.

My work spans instructional design, program management, and learning strategy. I've designed onboarding programs that moved measurable business outcomes, built custom interactive courses from scratch, and led facilitation for over 100 trainees across multiple classroom waves.

More recently, I've been teaching myself to code well enough to build interactive learning experiences that don't exist in any standard authoring tool — pH-responsive sliders, custom stepped scenarios, live embedded demos. The gin course on the Work page is the most complete version of that experiment so far.

Outside work: photography, travel, and running a small photography brand with my partner.
