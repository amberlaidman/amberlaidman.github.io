Career Studio

Your own private place to practice for interviews with Claude.

Here's how to get your own copy of the practice space I use. It starts out empty, so plan on about 25 minutes to add a little about yourself and a few stories from your work.

1How it works

Career Studio is a page that lives in your own Claude account. It starts out empty, and the feedback is only as useful as what you give it, so getting started has two parts.

  1. Get your copy. In step 4, you download a template, and Claude turns it into your own private page.
  2. Add your information. The first time you open your page, a Getting started page walks you through filling in About me and adding three to five stories from your work. Claude reads these before every piece of feedback, so it fits you instead of being generic.

Once that's done, here's what you can use it for:

Interview
Practice questions with a score and feedback, follow-up questions like a real interviewer would ask, a self-intro to rehearse, your story bank, job descriptions you've saved with the stories that fit each one, and a Progress page.
Memo
Short written proposal prompts, like the take-home exercises some companies give, with feedback on your writing.
Companies
Profiles of companies you're interviewing with, so you can practice for a specific one.
Coaching
A place to talk through a career decision, or rehearse a hard conversation (like negotiating an offer) where Claude plays the other person.

A quick, honest note: this is a practice tool. The feedback comes from Claude, so it can be wrong or miss something, and it can't promise you a job. You decide what to keep. It also never makes up details for your stories: anything new it notices is shown to you first, and you choose whether to save it.

2Before you start

Check these few things first so setup goes smoothly:

  • A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Your page saves your work and asks Claude for feedback, and I set mine up on a paid plan. Not confirmed: whether every part works on the free plan, and plans and features can vary by country.
  • A regular chat, not incognito. Incognito chats can't create files, so they can't make your page.
  • "Artifacts," "AI-powered artifacts" and "Cloud code execution and file creation" turned on. They're usually on already, and you can check in Settings → Capabilities.
  • A computer for setup, if you can. You'll download one file and attach it to a chat, which is easiest on a computer, and after that your page works on your phone too.
3Your privacy choices

Your page will hold your work history and your stories, so it's worth deciding a couple of things before you start.

Before you paste the prompt

  1. How your chats may be used. You choose whether your chats help improve Claude. Use the Model Improvement setting in your Privacy Settings, and turn it off first if you prefer.
  2. Memory. If you'd rather keep this setup out of Claude's memory, turn off "Generate memory from chats" in the Memory section of your settings before you start. Don't use an incognito chat for this, because incognito can't make your page.

After setup

  • Your page starts private. Only you can open it until you share it.
  • Your saved work is set to owner only. The starter prompt asks Claude to set up your page so only you can read or change what's saved in it. Not confirmed: I haven't tested what someone else sees if you share the link, so I'd keep the link to yourself.
  • Your data is yours. Use Download full backup in your page's Settings to save a copy of everything.

From Claude's Help Center and Privacy Center. Links are in the Help tab.

4Get your copy

You'll download a blank copy of my Career Studio, with none of my information in it, and Claude turns it into your own private page.

  1. Download the template with the button below. Your browser may ask you to confirm first, and the file is called career-studio-template.html.
  2. Copy the starter prompt.
  3. Open a new regular chat in Claude, attach the template file, and paste the prompt in.
  4. Open your new page. Claude sends you a link, and it's worth bookmarking since that's where you'll go every time.
  5. Allow Claude to help on the page. The first time you ask for feedback, Claude asks for permission, so select Allow.

5Your first visit

The first time you open your page, it shows a short Getting started page with three steps. Until you've finished all three, Home shows a small progress bar with a button for your next step, and it goes away once you're done.

  1. Fill in About me. Tell it your name, the roles you're going for, where you are right now and how you like to be coached. Claude reads this before every piece of feedback so it fits you, and you can change it anytime in Settings.
  2. Add your stories. Write three to five stories from your work in Stories, which are the examples you'll practice telling, so they don't need to be perfect yet.
  3. Practice your first question. Go to Interview, then Practice, and select Get a question. Type or dictate your answer and select Get feedback.

About the starter content: your page comes with practice questions, memo prompts and company profiles already in it. I made them for roles in learning and development, enablement and customer education, so if you're in a different field, ask Claude to swap them for ones that fit you (see step 7).

6Using it week to week

There's no right way to use it, but here's a rhythm that works well when you're actively interviewing:

  • A few practice questions a week. Select Ask me a follow-up after feedback, the way a real interviewer would dig in, and Ask another to keep going. When you're finished, select I'm done, then Save what's new to my story, and you'll get to check each detail before anything is saved.
  • Before an interview, open Companies, read the profile, then practice with that company selected so the questions fit them.
  • When you find a job you want, go to the Practice tab, choose A new job description under Practice for, and paste the posting. Claude shows which of your stories fit what it asks for and where you may want a new one, and once you save it, your practice questions follow that posting.
  • If a company gives you a written exercise, try a few Memo prompts first with the timer on.
  • When something's on your mind, like an offer or which direction to go next, use Coaching. Talk lets you think out loud with Claude, and Practice lets you rehearse the conversation itself.
  • Open Progress now and then to check your scores over time and which themes still need a story.

Once a week: open Settings in your page and select Download full backup, and if anything ever goes wrong, you can put everything back with Restore from a full backup.

7Changes and updates

To change your content, go back to the chat where you made your page (or start a new one and paste your page's link) and ask Claude. For example:

  • "Add a company profile for [company] to my Career Studio, using only the company's own official pages."
  • "Replace the starter practice questions in my Career Studio with ones for [your field]."
  • "Add three memo prompts for [type of role]."

When I share a newer version of the template, you can update your page without losing anything. Download a backup first, then attach the new file to the chat where you made your page and ask Claude, "Publish this new version to my existing Career Studio page, at the same link, with the same capabilities as before." Your saved work stays with the page.

Not confirmed: I've only tested updates on my own page so far.

Troubleshooting

Need help? Claude can help you troubleshoot. Go back to the chat where you made your page, since that chat already knows your setup. If you can't find it, start a new chat and include your page's link. Copy the message below, fill in the parts in brackets, and send it.

Help message

My Career Studio page isn't working the way I expected.

My page's link: [paste your page's link here if this is a new chat]
What I was trying to do: [for example, get feedback on a practice answer]
What happened instead: [describe it, or paste the exact message you got]

Please help me troubleshoot this one step at a time, in simple words.

Setup

The template didn't download.
Downloads ask you to confirm first, so look for a prompt and select Save. If nothing happens, select Open the file instead and save the page from your browser, or ask the person who shared this guide to send you the file.
Claude says it can't make the page.
Check that you're on a paid plan, in a regular chat (not incognito), and that "Artifacts," "AI-powered artifacts" and "Cloud code execution and file creation" are on in Settings → Capabilities. Then start a new chat, attach the file again and paste the prompt.
Claude wants to rebuild or change the page.
Reply, "Please publish the attached file exactly as it is, without changes." The page already does everything; it only needs to be published.

Using your page

Feedback says Claude's features aren't available.
Open your page in the Claude app or on claude.ai, signed in to your own account. The first time you ask for feedback, Claude asks for permission to help on the page. Select Allow. If you declined by mistake, reload the page and try again.
I've reached a usage limit.
Feedback, follow-ups, memos and coaching all use your own Claude plan. Wait a while and try again. What you wrote stays in the box.
My draft disappeared.
Drafts you haven't gotten feedback on are saved only in that browser on that device, so switching devices or clearing browser data can erase them. Anything you got feedback on is saved to your account.
Something I saved is missing.
Reload the page first. If it's still missing, open Settings in your page and use Restore from a full backup. Restoring only puts things back; it never deletes anything.
The questions or companies don't fit my field.
Ask Claude to swap them. Step 7 of the Guide has example messages you can copy.

There's more help inside your page, too, under Settings → Help and troubleshooting. For account or billing questions, visit Claude's Help Center.

Your privacy choices are in step 3 of the Guide: Model Improvement, memory, and who can see your page. It's best to make them before you paste the prompt.

The demo on this page keeps whatever you type in your own browser only. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere, and Start over clears it.
