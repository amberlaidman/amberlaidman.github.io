How to dictate your answer
Answer out loud the way you would in the interview, and let dictation type it into this box. Select the box first, then:
  • Mac: press the microphone key in the top row or the globe (fn) key twice, or choose Edit, then Start Dictation, and if nothing happens, turn on Dictation in System Settings under Keyboard.
  • Windows: press the Windows key and H together.
  • Phone: select the microphone on your keyboard.
Press the same keys again to stop, and before you get feedback, check any names of tools or companies, since dictation often mishears them. On a Mac, dictation stops after a long pause, so select the end of your text and start it again to keep going.

Welcome to Career Studio

This is your private place to practice interviews and proposal memos, look up companies, and get coaching, and it works best once it knows a little about you, so the steps below set it up in about 15 minutes, and you can stop anytime.

When you're ready, explore the rest from Home: practice a self-intro, write a proposal memo, look up a company's values, or talk something through with your coach.

You can come back to this page from the progress bar on Home or from Settings.

Settings and help

About me

 Claude reads this before every piece of feedback and every coaching session, so it knows who you are and how you want to be helped, and only you can see it.

Backup and restore

 Save a copy of everything in Career Studio to your device now and then and before big changes, using the full backup if you might need to restore it and the stories file if you just want something easy to read.
This puts back everything from a full backup file, replacing anything with the same name with the backup's version without deleting anything else.

Help and troubleshooting
Claude says its features aren't available in this view

Open Career Studio in the Claude app or on claude.ai, signed in to your own account. The first time you use feedback or coaching, Claude asks for permission to help on this page, so select Allow, and if you declined by mistake, reload the page and try again.

I've reached a usage limit

Feedback, follow-ups, memos, and coaching all use your own Claude plan. When you hit a limit, wait a while and try again. Nothing you wrote is lost; your draft stays in the box.

Dictation stopped partway through

On a Mac, dictation stops after a long pause. Select the end of your text and start dictation again. Full steps for Mac, Windows, and phone are under "How to dictate your answer" on the Practice page.

The feedback mentions something I didn't say, or gets a name wrong

Dictation sometimes mishears names of tools or companies, so check your answer before getting feedback. If something still seems wrong, select Get feedback again. Feedback never adds facts to your stories on its own; anything new shows up in a list for you to check first.

My draft disappeared

Drafts you haven't gotten feedback on are saved only in this browser on this device, so switching devices or using a private window can leave them behind and clearing your browser data erases them, while anything you got feedback on is saved to your account and shows up everywhere.

Something I saved is missing

Reload the page first. If it's still missing, use Restore from a full backup above. Restoring only puts items back or replaces them with the backup's version; it never deletes anything.

The download didn't happen

Downloads ask you to confirm first. Find the prompt and select Save. If nothing appears, another download prompt may still be open.

How do I change company profiles or my positioning?

Ask Claude in chat to update them. Your coaching context and stories can be edited right on the page.

Who can see my information?

Only you. Everything in Career Studio is private to your account, even if someone else opens the link.

What do the scores mean?

Every score is 1 to 3: 3 is strong, 2 is partly there, and 1 is missing or weak. Each practice type has its own rubric, so scores are only compared within the same type.

The page looks out of date

Reload the page to get the newest version.
